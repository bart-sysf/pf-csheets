How to calculate ability modifiers?

modifier = floor((score − 10) ÷ 2)

Mod. −1 +0 +1 +2 +3 +4 +5

Basic check logic

d20 + relevant modifiers ≥ DC / AC / CMD
Nat 20 and nat 1 auto hit and miss on attack rolls, trigger critical hits or fumbles; auto succeed/fail saving throws and CMB checks, except for escaping. Ability and skill checks have no general automatic 20/1 rule.

Beware the feats (and the GM)

Almost anything in this reference is subject to influence of character feats, traits, special abilities, etc. Those (and your GM) should take precedence.

Combat & Movement

Pathfinder first edition · Table reference

Turn basics

Regular turn: standard + move or full-round, plus swift/free actions. A standard can become a move. A 5-foot step forbids other movement, but does not provoke. An immediate action uses your next swift and is unavailable while flat-footed.

Surprise round: occurs only when some, but not all, combatants are aware. Roll initiative; aware combatants take one standard or move action, plus free actions. Unaware combatants do not act. Everyone is flat-footed until their first turn unless an ability says otherwise. If everyone or no one is aware, skip the surprise round.

Common free actions

Cease concentrating on a spell; drop an item; drop prone; prepare ordinary spell components or a focus; speak briefly; release a grapple. Drawing ammunition is part of the attack, not a separate action. Free actions generally do not provoke, and the GM may limit how many are reasonable. Speaking can occur outside your turn.

Attacks of opportunity (AoOs)

Usually provokes Usually does not
Leave a threatened square, including moving within threatened area Enter a threatened square; 5-foot step
Cast a spell normally; use a spell-like ability Cast defensively and succeed on concentration check
Ranged attack, including a ranged touch attack Melee attack, melee touch attack
Drink a potion; use a scroll; retrieve a stored item Drop a held item; draw a weapon; speak briefly
Stand from prone; reload many ranged weapons Withdraw from the starting square; other squares may provoke
Most maneuvers against their target Maneuvers with relevant Improved feat

Threatened area: adjacent squares, including diagonals. A typical reach weapon threatens 10 ft but not adjacent squares. Unarmed creatures generally do not threaten unless an ability says otherwise.

Movement exceptions: Acrobatics can avoid movement AoOs (check against opponent CMD). A 5-foot step allows no other movement in the round and requires suitable terrain. Withdraw protects only the starting square; cannot withdraw when blinded.

Limit: 1 AoO per round, even with many triggers. Only one AoO per opportunity, and movement from multiple threatened squares by one opponent is one opportunity. Flat-footed creatures cannot make AoOs.

Check in order: Does the enemy threaten your square with a melee attack? Does your action provoke? Can they make an AoO now? If yes, resolve one melee attack before the provoking action finishes.

Combat maneuvers

Maneuver Use / common action
Bull rush Push target 5 ft, +5 ft for each 5 by which you exceed CMD; standard action or part of a charge. Max. 1 size larger.
Dirty trick Impose a brief condition (blinded, dazzled, deafened, entangled, shaken, sickened) for 1 round, +1 round for every 5 by which you exceed CMD; standard action.
Disarm Make target drop a held item; replaces a melee attack. Self unarmed? −4 penalty. Choose one item for target to drop. Succeed by ≥10: target drops items in both hands. Fail by ≥10: drop your weapon.
Drag Pull target 5 ft, +5 ft for each 5 by which you exceed CMD; standard action. Max. 1 size larger.
Grapple Grab target as a standard action; grapple actions on later rounds follow their own rules. Humanoid take −4 penalty on attempt if no free hands. +5 to maintain the grapple if target does not break it. Escaping with own CMB or Escape Artist ≥ grappler CMD. Both target and grappler gain the grappled condition.
Overrun Move through target; exceed CMD by 5 to knock it prone. Max. 1 size larger. Add +2 to DC for each leg the target has above 2.
Reposition Move a foe 5 ft within your reach, +5 ft for each 5 by which you exceed CMD; standard action. Max. 1 size larger.
Steal Take an item that is not held, closely worn (like armor or rings) nor hidden in a bag or pack; standard action. You must have 1 hand free. Fastened items (like cloaks or sheathed weapons) give a +5 bonus to opponent CMD.
Sunder Damage a held/carried object; replaces a melee attack. Reducing to less than 0: you can choose to destroy the item or leave broken with 1 hp.
Trip Knock target prone; replaces a melee attack. Max. 1 size larger. Fail by ≥10: you fall prone. Add +2 to DC for each leg the target has above 2.
Tie Up Bind an already grappled target with a rope. Requires CMB check with a −10 penalty. DC to escape is 20 + grappler CMB.
Pin Pin an already grappled target. You keep the grappled condition and lose DEX bonus to AC.

Provocation

A maneuver provokes from its target. If the target's AoO hits, subtract the damage dealt from your maneuver check. Individual maneuvers have limits and exceptions; check their text.

How to roll for maneuvers?

d20 + CMB ≥ target CMD
Bonuses to attack rolls generally apply to CMB.

Attack rules and modifiers

Situation Quick rule
Aid another Combat: standard action, melee attack vs AC 10 → ally +2 on next attack against that foe or +2 AC vs that foe’s next attack. Skills: DC 10 skill check → +2 on ally’s check where aiding is possible.
Ammunition ★ House rule: Ordinary arrows and bolts are unlimited and need not be purchased.
Charge Full-round action: move at least 10 ft. and up to 2× speed, then make one melee attack (+2 attack; −2 AC until your next turn). Move directly toward the target along a clear path; no difficult terrain, obstacles, or creatures in the way (helpless creatures don’t block). You need line of sight at the start and must end in the nearest space from which you can attack. No 5-ft. step that round. If restricted to one standard action, you may charge up to your speed instead. Draw a weapon during the charge only if you move no farther than your speed and have BAB +1 (or Quick Draw on a standard-action charge). A charge bull rush gets +2 CMB.
Concealment After a successful attack roll, roll d100: 21–100 hits with 20% concealment; 51–100 with total concealment (50%). Total concealment also prevents directly targeting a creature with an attack.
Coup de grace Full-round action against a helpless foe; automatic critical hit. If defender survives, they make a Fortitude save DC 10 + damage dealt or die anyway. Provokes AoOs.
Critical hits ★ House rule: Critical threats that hit apply immediately; natural 1 fumbles also apply immediately. No confirmation roll. Draw from the table’s online card deck. Roll weapon damage with a multiplier one step lower (×2 → ×1; ×3 → ×2), then apply the card effect.
DR / resistance DR subtracts from qualifying weapon/physical attack damage unless bypassed; energy resistance subtracts from matching energy damage per attack.
Fighting defensively −4 attacks; +2 dodge AC (+3 with 3+ Acrobatics ranks).
Flanking +2 melee attack when you and a threatening ally are on opposite sides/corners.
Ranged into melee −4 attack when target is in melee with an ally; −2 if target is 2 size categories larger than allies; none if 3+ categories larger or with Precise Shot.
Total defense Standard action; +4 dodge AC (+6 with 3+ Acrobatics ranks); cannot attack or make AoOs.

Cover vs. concealment

Cover is a physical obstruction in the attack’s path.

Concealment is obscured sight and gives a 20% miss chance after a hit. Total concealment blocks sight, but you may attack a guessed square with a 50% miss chance.

Death & dying

HP / state What happens
0 hp · Disabled Choose one move or standard action per round (not both); swift, immediate, and free actions are allowed. Half speed. A standard or other strenuous action deals 1 hp damage after it resolves.
Negative hp above −CON score · Dying Unconscious. Starting on your next turn and each turn after, attempt a DC 10 Constitution check to stabilize, with a penalty equal to your negative hp. On a failed check, lose 1 hp; at −CON score, you die. You can also be stabilized by (healing) spells or a DC 15 Heal check from another.
Stable below 0 Unconscious and no longer losing hp. Each hour, attempt a DC 10 Constitution check (penalty equal to negative hp) to wake as disabled. If you stabilized without aid, a failed check costs 1 hp.
−CON hp or CON 0 · Dead Also if killed outright. Cannot benefit from normal or magical healing; only life-restoring magic can help.
Unconscious (other causes) Helpless; can also result when nonlethal damage exceeds current hp.

Rest and long-term care

Rest Natural recovery
8 hours 1 hp per character level; 1 point of ability damage per affected ability score.
24 hours complete bed rest 2 hp per character level; 2 points per affected ability score.
Successful long-term care (Heal DC 15) With 8 hours’ rest: 2 hp/level and 2 ability points per affected score. With 24 hours’ bed rest: 4 hp/level and 4 points. Healer provides 8 hours’ light activity.

Spellcasting

Pathfinder first edition · Table reference

Concentration checks

Check logic

d20 + caster level + casting ability modifier + other bonuses ≥ DC

Use the ability that grants bonus spells for that class (typically INT, WIS, or CHA). Failure loses the spell.

When to roll concentration?

Usually roll only when you choose defensive casting or something disrupts the spell. Spell-created weather uses its own rules.

While falling, spellcasting is normally possible only for a fall over 500 ft. or an immediate-action spell.

Fire spells underwater also need a caster-level check (DC 20 + spell level).

Situation while casting Concentration DC
Defensively (avoid casting AoO) 15 + 2 × spell level
Damaged / injured 10 + damage dealt + spell level
Continuous damage 10 + ½ the continuous damage taken last round + spell level
Grappled or pinned 10 + grappler’s CMB + spell level
Entangled 15 + spell level
Distracted by nondamaging spell Disrupting spell’s save DC (or 10 + its level + its caster ability mod if no save) + level being cast
Vigorous / violent / extreme motion Vigorous 10; violent 15; extreme 20 + spell level
Wind with rain or sleet 5 + spell level
Wind with hail, dust, or debris 10 + spell level
Underwater (cannot breathe water) 15 + spell level
While falling (when casting is possible) 20 + spell level
Example: Level 5 wizard (INT 18) casting a 3rd-level spell defensively: d20 + 5 + 4 vs. DC 21; a roll of 12 or higher succeeds. If hit for 8 damage while casting it, the DC is also 21.

Counterspells

Method Quick rule
Match the spell Ready a standard action for when the target starts casting. Spellcraft (free action, DC 15 + spell level) identifies it; if identified and you can cast that same spell, cast it as the readied action to automatically counter it (if in range). Arcane and divine versions work; metamagic does not matter.
Dispel magic (spell) Ready it to counter without identifying the spell; succeed at a caster-level check d20 + CL ≥ 11 + enemy CL.

Casting & attacks of opportunity

Situation Quick rule
Casting while threatened Usually provokes an AoO. If it hits and damages you, or otherwise disrupts the spell, check concentration.
Cast defensively Choose before casting; success avoids the casting AoO, failure loses the spell.
Touch spell Casting the spell provokes; delivering a held melee touch spell does not. A ranged touch attack provokes as a ranged attack.
Spell-like ability Usually provokes and can be used defensively; use its effective spell level for the check.
Supernatural ability Usually does not provoke and is not disrupted like a spell; check the ability text.

Casting time & interruption

Spell timing What to remember
1 standard action Usually resolves during your turn; later damage cannot interrupt a completed spell.
1 round Full-round casting; completes just before your next turn. Keep concentrating until then.
Longer than 1 round Requires continued casting/concentration; interruption can lose the spell. Often 1 minute (= 10 rounds of full-round concentration).
1 swift action Only once per round, but does not count against 1 spell per round limit.

Spell resistance

d20 + caster level + bonuses ≥ SR
Roll only if the spell lists SR: yes; meeting or exceeding the target’s SR lets the spell affect it.

Spellcasting terms

Term Quick explanation
Spell level Sets spell-slot level and contributes to save DC; use the level for your class.
Caster level (CL) Usually class level; often sets range, duration, damage, and checks against spell resistance / dispel.
Save DC 10 + spell level + casting ability modifier
Components V = spoken; S = precise gesture; M = material (usually consumed); F = reusable focus; DF = divine focus.
Touch attack Use your normal melee / ranged attack bonus against touch AC.
Prepared / spontaneous Prepared spells are expended when cast; spontaneous casters spend a slot but keep the spell known. Class features can differ.

Bonus stacking

Bonus / penalty Stacking rule
Different types Usually stack.
Same type Usually use only the highest bonus.
Exceptions Dodge and most circumstance bonuses stack; untyped bonuses usually stack unless from the same source. Penalties usually stack.

Damage reduction (DR)

Notation / rule Quick reminder
DR 5/magic Subtract 5 from each qualifying physical hit unless the weapon is magic (+1 or better).
DR X/— Subtract X from physical attacks; no bypass type is listed.
What bypasses DR? Follow the slash: material, alignment, weapon type, or magic. Spells and energy damage ignore DR; if multiple DR values apply, use the best one.

Coins

The default coin type is gold pieces (gp). The more valuable type is platinum pieces (pp), they are worth 10 gp. Less valuable types are silver pieces (sp), worth 1/10 of a gp and one step lesser is copper pieces (cp), worth 1/10 of an sp.

50 coins, regardsless of type, weigh 1 lb.

Difficult terrain & hampered movement

Each square costs 2 squares of movement (3 for a diagonal move into it). You can’t run or charge across it. Applies to poor visibility as well. Obstacles might require an additional skill check.

Fear progression

Stage Effect
Shaken −2 on attacks, saves, skill checks, and ability checks.
Frightened Shaken; flee from the source if possible. If unable to flee, can fight.
Panicked Shaken; drop held items and flee at top speed along a random path, away from all dangers. No other actions except abilities needed to escape; cower if trapped.
Cowering Cornered: frozen in fear, no actions, −2 AC, and lose DEX bonus.

Fear escalates: shaken + shaken → frightened; shaken + frightened or frightened + shaken / frightened → panicked.

Option When / result
Take 10 Assume a d20 result of 10 when not threatened or distracted.
Take 20 Assume 20 after spending 20× as long, when not threatened or distracted; retries must be possible and failure must have no harmful consequence.

Falling

Falling: 1d6 damage per 10 ft., maximum 20d6; a fall causing lethal damage leaves you prone. Deliberate jump: first 1d6 is nonlethal. DC 15 Acrobatics avoids damage from the first 10 ft. and makes the next 10 ft. nonlethal.
